2. What VoxBridge does

The phone app captures audio with the microphone permission you grant. The desktop app receives that audio and writes it to an audio device you choose. When you pair a phone with a PC, the desktop may remember the device so you do not have to enter a code every time. Nothing is uploaded to us; there is no VoxBridge account and no VoxBridge relay in the audio path.

The website at voxbridge-app-pi.vercel.app is a marketing and documentation site. It stores anonymous download counters and messages you choose to send through the contact form. See the privacy policy for the details.

3. Consent and lawful use

Recording laws differ by country, state and situation. You are responsible for having any consent required before you capture another person's voice, whether in a call, a meeting, a classroom or a public space. VoxBridge is a microphone, not a hidden recorder: it does not start on its own, and both the phone and the desktop show a live indicator while audio is flowing. Do not use it to deceive people about being recorded.

4. The virtual audio cable is a separate product

VoxBridge can route audio into any virtual audio cable already installed on your PC. Our setup guide recommends VB-CABLE, which is created and licensed by VB-Audio Software, not by us. It is donationware: free for personal use, with its own licence terms for commercial use. VoxBridge does not ship VB-CABLE inside its installer; the app links to the official download and verifies that the device is present. Your relationship with VB-Audio is governed by their terms.

Builds are published on the project's GitHub releases page together with a SHA256SUMS.txt file. Verify the hash before running an installer if your threat model calls for it. Unless stated otherwise on the release, Windows builds are not code-signed, so SmartScreen may show a "Windows protected your PC" prompt. iOS builds are unsigned development archives and require your own Apple signing identity to install. Do not install a build from a source that is not this repository.
