VoxBridge / Privacy policy

Privacy policy, in full.

Short version: your voice stays on your network. This page is the long version, written to be read.

Effective 20 October 2026 · Version 1.1 · Applies to VoxBridge 1.0.1 and voxbridge-app-pi.vercel.app

VoxBridge is a local audio tool. The audio you speak is captured on your phone, transported directly to your PC over your own network, and written to a virtual audio device on that PC. It is never sent to us and there is no server in the path.

1. Who we are

VoxBridge is an independent open source project. References to “we”, “us” and “our” mean the VoxBridge maintainers. References to “you” mean the person using the app or website. The project has no logins, no customer database, and no advertising partners.

Contact for privacy questions: privacy@voxbridge.app.

2. The audio path, exactly

  1. The phone captures microphone audio with the operating system’s audio API.
  2. Audio is processed on the device by the VoxBridge DSP chain (cleanup, tone, dynamics, protection).
  3. It is framed and sent over a WebSocket to your PC on your local network — or, in USB mode, through a forwarded local port over the cable.
  4. The desktop app decodes it, applies the desktop chain, and writes it to the virtual audio cable you configured.
  5. Other applications on that PC, such as Zoom or OBS, read from the cable output.

Audio is never uploaded. There is no relay, no recording on a server, and no analytics that receives a sample. Session recordings are written only where you choose on your own disk.

4. What the website collects

The website is static, has no login, and sets no advertising cookies. It does four small things:

  • Anonymous download counting. When you click a download, we record the platform (Windows, Android or iOS), the app version and the source page. No IP address, no user agent, no identifier — the row is not linkable to you.
  • Anonymous page counting. Each page load records the page path, the domain of the site that referred you, and any utm_* campaign tag in the link you followed. No cookie, no browser identifier, no IP address — a row cannot be linked back to you, and nothing is shared with advertisers.
  • Optional feedback. If you send a message or subscribe to release notes, we store the email address you typed plus the message. That is the only personal data we knowingly hold.
  • Local theme preference. Light or dark is stored in your browser’s local storage. It never reaches a server.

Hosting is provided by Vercel and the database by Supabase. Like any web host, they process standard request metadata (IP address, timestamp, requested path) to deliver and protect the site. We do not combine that metadata with the anonymous page or download counters, and we never sell or share it.

5. What the apps never do

  • No account, sign-in, email verification or advertising identifier.
  • No crash reporting or analytics SDK is embedded in the desktop or mobile apps.
  • No access to contacts, photos, files, location, camera, clipboard or SMS.
  • No microphone use while the app is idle. Android shows the active microphone indicator whenever capture is running, and the foreground notification is always visible.
  • No hidden background network calls. The phone app only connects to the PC you paired with.

7. Security of local sessions

Sessions require a four digit pairing code that rotates after every connection, or a remembered token stored as a hash. Failed attempts are rate limited. The listener binds to your LAN only; it is not reachable from the internet unless you deliberately forward a port, which we do not recommend.

8. Children

VoxBridge is a general-purpose audio utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13; the app has no account system to do so.

9. Your rights

Because the project holds almost no personal data, most requests are simple. You can ask us to export or delete any feedback or release-notes email you sent. Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA), you may also have the right to object to processing, request correction, or lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. Email privacy@voxbridge.app; we answer within 30 days.

10. Retention

  • Feedback messages and release-note addresses: kept until the conversation is closed or you ask us to delete them, whichever comes first.
  • Anonymous download and page counters: aggregate only, retained for up to 12 months and then deleted, because a row cannot identify a person.
  • Server request logs at our hosts: subject to their standard short retention policies.

11. International transfers

Website hosting and the feedback database may process data in regions outside your country, including the United States and the European Union, under the standard contractual terms those providers offer. Audio is not part of any transfer because it never leaves your devices.

12. Third parties

  • Vercel — static hosting and CDN.
  • Supabase — feedback and the anonymous page and download counters, protected by row-level security.
  • GitHub — source code, release downloads and build logs. Downloading a release file is served by GitHub under its own privacy policy.
  • VB-Audio — only if you choose the guided virtual cable install. Your browser or the app requests the installer from vb-audio.com; we do not proxy or track it.

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with advertisers or data brokers. Ever.

13. Changes to this policy

Material changes will be published on this page with a new effective date and noted in the release notes. If a change would involve sending audio anywhere, it will be opt-in and off by default — that is a commitment, not a promise we can quietly reverse.

14. Contact

Privacy: privacy@voxbridge.app

Everything else: support page
